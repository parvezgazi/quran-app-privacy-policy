Privacy Policy for Al-Quran Kareem

Effective Date: August 1, 2026

Al-Quran Kareem ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles information when you use our services.

1. Information Collection & Usage

We do not require users to create an account or provide personal identifying information (such as name, email address, or phone number) to use the core features of the app.

2. App Permissions & Data Access

  • Location Access (GPS & Network): Used strictly on your device to calculate local Islamic Prayer Times and Qibla Direction compass. Location data is processed locally on your phone and is never stored on external servers or sold to third parties.
  • Internet & Network Access: Required to stream high-quality Quranic audio recitations and multi-language translations from global open public CDNs (api.alquran.cloud & Quran.com).
  • Storage Access: Used to save your reading progress, bookmarks, and app preferences locally on your phone using standard encrypted storage.

3. Advertising & Analytics (Third-Party Services)

Our app integrates Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and process non-personally identifiable device identifiers (such as Advertising ID) to serve personalized or contextual ads in compliance with Google's Advertising Policies.

For more information on how Google manages data in advertising, please review Google's Privacy & Terms Policy.

4. Children's Privacy

Our application contains Islamic religious content suitable for general audiences of all ages, including children. We strictly comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policies. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

5. Security

We value your trust in using our application. All app settings and user reading progress are stored locally on your device.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Any updates will be posted on this page.
